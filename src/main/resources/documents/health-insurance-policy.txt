HEALTH SECURE PLAN - POLICY OVERVIEW

This document describes the general terms of the Health Secure individual
health insurance plan.

Coverage amount: Up to Rs. 5,00,000 per policy year.

Premium: Rs. 1,200 per month, billed monthly. The premium may change at
renewal based on the insured's age band and claims history.

Policy term: Health Secure is issued as an annual policy and renews every
12 months, subject to the insurer's underwriting review at renewal.

What this plan covers, at a summary level:
- In-patient hospitalization expenses (see hospitalization-coverage.txt for
  full detail)
- Pre-hospitalization expenses for up to 30 days before admission
- Post-hospitalization expenses for up to 60 days after discharge
- Day-care procedures that do not require a 24-hour hospital stay
- Ambulance charges up to Rs. 2,000 per hospitalization

What this plan does not cover is listed in exclusions.txt. Claims are
subject to the process described in claim-process.txt.

This plan does not cover maternity expenses, cosmetic procedures, or
treatment outside India.
