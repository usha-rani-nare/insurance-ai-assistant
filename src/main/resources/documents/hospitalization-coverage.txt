HOSPITALIZATION COVERAGE DETAILS

This document explains what counts as covered hospitalization under the
Health Secure and Family Health Plus plans.

Minimum hospitalization period: A hospital stay must be at least 24
continuous hours to qualify as in-patient hospitalization, except for the
day-care procedures listed separately, which are covered without a minimum
stay requirement.

Covered hospitalization expenses include:
- Room rent, up to the room rent limit stated in the policy schedule
- ICU charges
- Doctor's fees and surgeon's fees
- Cost of medicines and consumables used during the hospital stay
- Diagnostic tests conducted during hospitalization

Room rent limit: Room rent is covered up to 1% of the sum insured per day.
If the insured chooses a room above this limit, associated charges
(doctor's fees, etc.) may be proportionately reduced, consistent with
standard health insurance practice.

Cashless hospitalization is available at network hospitals. For
non-network hospitals, the insured pays first and files a reimbursement
claim afterward - see claim-process.txt for how to do this.

A hospital stay of a few days for a covered illness (for example,
observation and treatment of dehydration or fever) is treated the same as
any other qualifying hospitalization: it is covered under in-patient
hospitalization, provided the illness itself is not on the exclusions list.
