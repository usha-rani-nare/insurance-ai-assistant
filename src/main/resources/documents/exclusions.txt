EXCLUSIONS

This document lists what is NOT covered under the Health Secure and Family
Health Plus plans. This list is illustrative for this demonstration project
and is not exhaustive.

Not covered under any circumstances:
- Cosmetic or plastic surgery, unless required to treat injuries from a
  covered accident
- Dental treatment, unless it requires hospitalization due to an accident
- Treatment received outside India
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Injuries or illness arising from participation in an adventure sport not
  declared at policy purchase

Not covered during the initial waiting period:
- Any illness (other than an accident) within the first 30 days of the
  policy starting
- Specific named conditions (for example, cataracts, hernia, and certain
  joint replacement surgeries) within the first 2 years of continuous
  coverage

Permanently excluded regardless of waiting period:
- Maternity and childbirth-related expenses (these require a separate
  maternity add-on, which is not part of this base plan)
- Experimental or unproven treatments
- Expenses for a hospital stay that does not meet the minimum
  hospitalization period described in hospitalization-coverage.txt, unless
  it is a listed day-care procedure

If a submitted claim relates to one of these exclusions, the correct
response is to clearly state that the treatment is not covered under the
policy, rather than guessing whether an exception might apply.
