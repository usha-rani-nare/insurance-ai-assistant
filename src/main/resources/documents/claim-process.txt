CLAIM PROCESS

This document describes how to file and track an insurance claim.

Step 1 - Notification: Inform the insurer of a planned hospitalization at
least 48 hours in advance where possible. For emergencies, notify within
24 hours of admission.

Step 2 - Cashless claims (network hospitals): The hospital's insurance
desk sends a pre-authorization request directly to the insurer. Once
approved, the hospital bills the insurer directly for covered expenses,
and the insured pays only for non-covered items, if any.

Step 3 - Reimbursement claims (non-network hospitals or where cashless was
not used): The insured pays the hospital directly, then submits a claim
with the following documents:
- Completed claim form
- Original hospital bills and payment receipts
- Discharge summary
- Doctor's prescriptions and diagnostic reports

Step 4 - Claim review: The insurer reviews the submitted claim and
supporting documents. A claim may be marked UNDER_REVIEW while this
happens.

Step 5 - Decision: The claim is either APPROVED (fully or partially, based
on policy limits and any applicable deductions) or REJECTED (for example,
if the treatment falls under an exclusion). Approved reimbursement claims
are typically settled within 15-30 working days of receiving complete
documentation.

Claim status values used in this system: SUBMITTED, UNDER_REVIEW,
APPROVED, REJECTED, SETTLED.

If a claim is rejected, the insured can request the specific reason for
rejection and, where applicable, submit additional documentation for
reconsideration.
